01 · Faction progression & beef limits

Build your faction identity, connections, rivalries, and storylines through roleplay before moving into larger conflicts.

  • Tier 1: focus on establishing your faction and building roleplay with others.
  • Tier 1.5: may hold active beef with up to three other Tier 1.5 factions at one time.
  • Tier 2: the three-beef limit is removed; conflicts may involve Tier 1.5 factions and above, subject to all other ROE.
02 · Faction attacks & attack types

Once accepted into a weapon scheme, faction attacks use firearms unless an exception is stated. Escalation must stay realistic and organic.

  • Deathmatching attacks: firearm faction attacks include firing in the air, at a person, or on set turf. Four or more opposing members/users on their set turf can qualify. The source lists a three-day cooldown and also says Tier 2+ may conduct two per day; see the source note below.
  • Robberies: six-hour cooldown. A robbery on another faction’s turf counts as an attack. Initiate naturally with a clear advantage; examples include 2v1 or 4v2. Do not force a robbery at even or near-even numbers such as 4v3 or 4v4. Unknown or naturally skewed numbers are treated with context.
  • Hostile environment: entering turf to provoke hostility may include chasing, putting someone into Death Mode, disrespecting the set, repeated spinning, instigating, or heavily trooping through. If the other faction reacts, the faction that created the hostile environment is treated as the attacker and receives a 12-hour Deathmatching Attack cooldown.
  • Mutual combat: both factions have five or more members in the same area and an organic interaction creates hostility that could escalate. The first faction to engage is the attacker. If a shootout occurs, both sides receive a 12-hour Deathmatching Attack cooldown. Mutual combat cannot take place on set turf.
Source wording to verify: the linked ROE mentions both a three-day Deathmatching Attack cooldown and two attacks per day for Tier 2+. Confirm how Faction Management applies these statements before relying on either limit.
03 · Attack requirements & conduct
  • Develop your character realistically toward obtaining a firearm, illegal activity, and a genuine negative interaction. Gunplay should not start the character’s story.
  • Wait at least 72 hours after creating a character or joining a faction before participating in an attack.
  • A maximum of four members may intentionally take part, except where turf-defense or mutual-combat exceptions apply.
  • Intentionally kill no more than three opposing members in an attack. An accidental fourth victim may be revived; there is no refund. If both sides actively engage in a shootout, characters are not revived.
  • Keep turf defense realistic: no extended roadblocks, constant patrols solely to defend turf, rooftop camping, or other tactics judged unrealistic or playing to win.
  • A double attack is allowed only with sufficient RP and if the opposing faction has not slid back within 120 hours. Wait five full calendar days before another Deathmatching Attack. This double-attack rule is for Deathmatching Attacks, not robberies.
  • Provide a shooter’s POV with sound when requested. It must be at least three minutes long and recorded with NVIDIA/GeForce Experience or AMD software.
  • Do not linger around an opposing turf without legitimate RP after an interaction. Faction Management decides whether conduct is turf camping.
  • Non-participants may not scout, spin, or relay information about a turf for attacks, defense, backdoor RP (except the person actively lining the victim), or future interactions. This applies on foot and in vehicles.
  • Do not attack probationary factions or involve them in gunplay, setups, or lines. A limited exception may apply if they directly provoke the situation, such as disrespecting deceased members.
  • For tiered/non-probationary factions, fist fights count as interactions, not attacks. During probation, an outnumbered physical altercation can count as an attack.
  • Non-faction members cannot join faction attacks except through the Illegal Civilian Scheme with sufficient evidence and established backdoor RP.
  • A masked opposing member can still be held liable. Do not use electric vehicles or motorcycles in faction attacks; mopeds and Citi Bikes are exceptions.
  • Remain in the server for at least 30 minutes after starting or defending an attack.
04 · Bullying, courtesy & tier boundaries
  • Tier 2 loot may only be used against Tier 1 or higher factions during an attack.
  • Tier 1.5 factions may use no more than two weapons when engaging a non-tiered faction.
  • Build roleplay before pursuing higher-tier status. Established factions should behave with reasonable respect and awareness of differences in experience, resources, and influence.
  • Violations may result in faction discipline even without a formal report.
05 · Pinpointing & escalation
  • Base pinpointing only on legitimate in-character information: known hangouts, homes, vehicles, clothing, members, or other details learned through RP. Nametags may count when reasonably discovered through the situation.
  • Attacks need meaningful roleplay, reason, and escalation. Aim for at least seven meaningful interactions with the person involved; do not manufacture filler interactions just to reach the number.
  • A faction member needs at least seven meaningful negative interactions with an opposing member before a shootout. Simple arguments do not count. This requirement is void if gunplay has already occurred between those players.
  • If an identified opposing faction member enters set turf, engage through RP. Deliberately refusing legitimate RP to escape or gain an advantage may be playing to win. Lethal action is only permitted when intentional avoidance is clear; do not use it to bypass escalation rules.
06 · Additional faction rules
  • Death Script and CK/PK outcomes depend on how the character was injured. A one-time PK may be available only if the character was not shot in the head/neck and did not take more than two chest gunshot wounds. PK injuries must be roleplayed for 12–72 hours of active in-game time; offline time does not count. Server-side issues are handled at administrator discretion.
  • Backdoor RP must grow from meaningful, realistic in-character investigation and development, not a thin one-line reason for revenge. The person lining someone is excluded from the four-person limit unless they use a weapon.
  • Brawls are limited to eight people per faction, including allies. Advertised public gatherings are not places to start beef, shoot, or create drama; move conflict elsewhere and develop it through RP.
  • Do not use Local OOC to avoid a loss or interrupt roleplay. Do not take inventory items from someone after knocking them out; physical belongings may be taken only through proper /me and /do roleplay.
  • Kidnapping is part of robbery; kidnapping to kill is not allowed, including through torture roleplay. Surrounded or cornered characters must value their life and show appropriate fear, even if armed.
  • F-Chat is for out-of-character announcements from High Council or faction leadership only. In-character use or metagaming may result in a faction strike.
  • Water evading to escape police or an opposing faction is prohibited and may result in automatic CK. Cop baiting to avoid jail or abuse server limits is prohibited.
  • Unregistered firearm serial numbers may lead to a ban or faction strike. A faction may have no more than five Untrusted Members.
  • NPC-owned stores on faction turf are not Greenzones under ROE. Player-owned businesses on turf remain Greenzones. Use courtesy toward players using NPC stores for their intended purpose.
  • Immediate self-defense may be allowed when placed in imminent danger, such as a gun being drawn and aimed or an intentional vehicle ram. These situations do not count as an attack for either side, subject to Faction Management discretion.
  • Firing a weapon anywhere puts you under a universal 12-hour Player Cooldown before another shootout. This does not apply when acting in defense.
  • Factions must have an active thread before formally establishing faction activity. Serious misconduct, poor representation, playing to win, or repeated greenzone provocation can lead to discipline, strike, or closure at management’s discretion.
07 · Reports & open reports
  • Submit faction reports within 24 hours of the incident. Do not file reports only to retaliate because a situation went badly.
  • While a report is open, both factions must avoid negative interactions with each other until the verdict is posted.
  • Public bashing or OOC toxicity toward another faction may lead to punishment. Use a report or leave the situation.
  • Faction Management may restrict firearm or drug sales while a faction report is pending.
08 · Weapon trading & refills
  • Weapon purchases and trades begin at Tier 1.5. The source says Tier 1.5 may sell one firearm weekly, then separately lists up to two weekly firearm sales for Tier 1.5 and three for Tier 2. See the source note below.
  • Log every firearm gained, purchased, traded, sold, or lost in faction Discord and report transactions to Faction Management. Record refills in a dedicated channel; lost firearms may be documented in the designated channel.
  • Weapon totals count toward the faction’s overall limit, including firearms received through refills.
  • Do not go inactive between refill periods just to return for more weapons. Abuse or manipulation of the scheme may lead to an immediate de-tier or other discipline.
Source wording to verify: the linked ROE gives two different Tier 1.5 weekly sale limits. Ask Faction Management which limit is current.

Shared tier boundaries

Build conflict through meaningful in-character interactions. Seven meaningful interactions and seven negative interactions are stated as general thresholds in the ROE.
